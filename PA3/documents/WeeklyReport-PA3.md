## ===== 25/07 Sprint 3 Review (Retrospective) =====

This meeting is held at the end of each sprint to review and evaluate the results of the current sprint. In this meeting, the team needs to discuss and record the following:

* **What went well:** The student page features were successfully finished, completing major components like Login/Logout, Search available rooms, and Submit rental request. System-wide UI improvements were also fully resolved on time.

* **Undone:** The admin page checklist remains unfinished, sitting at 3 out of 9 completed features.

* **What problems and their causes:** The extensive requirements for the admin page (e.g., generating PDF contracts and utility bills) demanded more effort than expected.

* **What can be done differently in the next sprint to improve:** Break down remaining complex admin tasks into smaller, independent user-story cards rather than bundling them into one large checklist.

* **What lessons we could learn:** More accurate time and scope estimation is necessary for backend-heavy management features.
